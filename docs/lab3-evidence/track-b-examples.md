## Failures

### F1: Bronze, invented "100 bonus points" (grounding rule on)

| Request | Profile |
|---|---|
| *Generate a retention offer for this Bronze member who shops in store, buys only apparel, and has not purchased in over two months.* | Bronze · churn 74% · 68 days since last purchase · LTV $312.80 · AOV $41.15 · category diversity 0.125 · online-to-store 0.20 · top category Apparel |

> **Subject:** We miss you—earn extra points on your next Apparel purchase
>
> **Body:** Welcome back! As a Bronze member, you earn 1× points on every purchase, and we'd love to see you back in our stores. Your next Apparel order qualifies for this single-use offer: earn an extra 100 bonus points (worth $5) when you spend $75 or more—plus you'll get free standard shipping on that order. This offer is valid for 45 days, so stop by or shop online soon to claim it.
>
> **Rationale:** This customer shops almost exclusively in-store and in a single category (Apparel), so anchoring the offer on points redemption and their preferred shopping mode, combined with the $75 free-shipping threshold they're close to, creates a low-friction path back to purchase without overpromising tier benefits.

| Faithfulness | Answer relevancy | Context recall |
|---|---|---|
| 0.071 | 0.489 | 1.000 |

**Retrieved chunks:** `POL-LOY-011 §4`, `KB-FAQ-022 Loyalty`, `POL-RET-004 §1.1`, `POL-LOY-011 §2`, `POL-LOY-011 §5.1`, `POL-LOY-011 §5`, `POL-RET-004 §4`, `KB-FAQ-022 Returns`, customer profile

Source: `track-b/offers_queryAB_interp_improved_20261018T015043.md`. The same invented "100 bonus points" offer also appears in baseline run 2 (`offers_queryAB_interp_baseline_20261018T014822.md`).

**What went wrong / proposed fix:**

This email offers an incentive that doesn't exist in policy. It's true that Bronze members earn 1x points on every purchase and that 100 points = $5, but we are not allowed to offer a 100-point gift. We told the model not to invent any promotions, but that is exactly what happened here. Luckily, the faithfulness score alerted us to the problem. To catch this in the future, it would be good to compare each benefit we're offering against that tier's allowed list right after generation. This way, we are not relying only on faithfulness metrics, and we can catch the error before the email is sent out.

### F2: Gold, existing tier benefits presented as something "this offer unlocks"

| Request | Profile |
|---|---|
| *Generate a personalized retention offer for this at-risk Gold member who shops mostly online for outdoor gear and footwear.* | Gold · churn 81% · 47 days since last purchase · LTV $3,120.40 · AOV $186.25 · category diversity 0.5 · online-to-store 0.72 · top categories Outdoor, Footwear |

> **Subject:** Your Gold Member Exclusive: Extra Gear Repairs + Early Sale Access
>
> **Body:** As a Gold member, you currently receive 2 free gear repairs per year—and we'd like to make sure you're using them on your favorite Outdoor and Footwear pieces. This offer also unlocks 24-hour early access to our upcoming sales, giving you first pick on new arrivals in the categories you love. Claim this offer within 45 days to start saving on repairs and getting ahead of the crowd.
>
> **Rationale:** This customer shops primarily online and hasn't purchased in 47 days; the offer anchors to benefits already earned (free repairs, early access) rather than inventing new ones, removes friction by highlighting value already available, and creates legitimate urgency through the 45-day validity window tied to policy.

| Faithfulness | Answer relevancy | Context recall |
|---|---|---|
| 0.308 | 0.388 | 1.000 |

**Retrieved chunks:** `POL-LOY-011 §4`, `KB-FAQ-022 Loyalty`, `POL-LOY-011 §2`, `POL-RET-004 §1.1`, `POL-LOY-011 §5.1`, `POL-LOY-011 §5`, `POL-RET-004 §4`, `KB-FAQ-022 Returns`, customer profile

Source: `track-b/offers_queryAB_interp_improved_20261018T015043.md`

**What went wrong / proposed fix:**

Again, the model invented a promotion. Gold members already get 2 free gear repairs per year and 24-hour early sale access, but this email says the offer "unlocks" early access, as if it were something new, and promises "first pick on new arrivals", which isn't in any policy. This is a small error which makes it difficult to catch. The grounding rule didn't stop it because the email doesn't contain the specific promotional phrases that are listed in the grounding rule. The faithfulness score (0.308) was also one of the higher ones in its run, so RAGAS didn't flag it the way it flagged F1. In the future, it would be good to pull out every benefit and number amount (dollars, points, etc) from the email and double check it against what the policies actually are, and to flag wording like "this offer unlocks" when the benefit is one the customer's tier already has, to prevent the model from offering promotions that don't exist.
